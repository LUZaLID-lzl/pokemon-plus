A small top-down overworld terrain map for a retro Pokemon handheld pixel game, landscape horizontal rectangle, 2:1 aspect ratio. View of a cozy Kanto countryside region: deep sage green forests around the perimeter, muted grassland clearings through center, dusty paths winding from southwest toward northern rocky mountain foothills, a small river flowing down the right side, tiny sea inlet in bottom left, a few rock clusters in north. Leave a broad clear grassland in upper center for a gym marker to be placed later. Clean bold square pixels, limited 24-color palette, muted low contrast forest greens and tan paths, dark blue outlines, very legible when reduced to 96x52 native pixels and scaled 3x. No text, no labels, no city names, no UI, no border, no characters, no Pokemon, no buildings, no icons, no circles, no glowing points.
